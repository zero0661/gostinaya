## Controller and purpose

I voluntarily consent to Peter Vladimirovich Milenin, an individual and author of After Login (milenin.pro), processing my data to request and confirm a subscription, send new essays in my chosen language, and manage unsubscription. Contact: milen.petr@gmail.com.

## Data

Email address, newsletter language and subscription status; request, confirmation and unsubscription information; consent identifier, document version and server timestamps; technical delivery information. Subscription data is not published.

## Processing

Automated collection, recording, organisation, storage, correction, retrieval, use, transfer to the technical provider for hosting and email delivery, restriction and deletion. The newsletter is not used for advertising or selling the subscriber list.

## Hosting and delivery

New operational databases and the consent journal are hosted in the Russian Yandex Cloud region ru-central1; new backups use Yandex Cloud Backup. Email is sent via Yandex Cloud Postbox. The technical provider is Yandex.Cloud LLC. My chosen email provider also processes delivered messages under its own terms and in its own locations.

## Confirmation and withdrawal

The initial service email contains a confirmation link valid for 24 hours. New essays are sent after the address is confirmed. Consent lasts until withdrawal or the end of the processing purpose. I can withdraw consent and request deletion by emailing milen.petr@gmail.com from my subscription address. Newsletter emails also contain an unsubscribe link followed by a separate confirmation button.

## Separate choice

Consent is given using a separate checkbox that is not checked in advance. It is not required to read the website, does not cover advertising, and is separate from registering in the Lounge. The privacy policy describes the processing arrangements separately.
